The women have the skill. The process is what’s confusing.

Millions of Self-Help Groups make bags, pickles, clothes and handicrafts. Most run an order from WhatsApp, phone calls, memory and a paper notebook. Six things go wrong, and each one has a fix.

Orders are unclearA voice note says “same as last time.” Size, colour and price are guesses.The order is read back and confirmed before work starts.
Pricing is a guessPrices follow habit or buyer pressure, so a low offer can mean a loss.Fixed code adds material, labour and overhead, and shows the floor price.
Work is shared by word of mouthNobody checks skills or free time, and a woman has no easy way to say no.Tasks are suggested with reasons. Anyone can decline, with no penalty.
Payout is not clearMembers can’t see how their pay was worked out, which breeds doubt and fights.Every pay slip shows pieces × rate. “I disagree” holds the payment.
Deadlines slip quietlyFalling behind is noticed when it is already too late.Checkpoints raise an alert, and the leader gets simple options.
Records are in someone’s headThere is nothing to show a buyer, a bank or the mission.A tamper-evident ledger records every decision.

Our rule: a machine never decides a woman’s pay.

How one order moves through Sakhi Khata

Every step is a short WhatsApp message and a live dashboard. Each person uses their own phone, and the dashboards update as the messages arrive.

  1. Order comes inThe buyer sends text or a voice note. AI picks out quantity, price and date, and flags what is unclear.Buyer
  2. Price checkExact decimal maths gives the floor price and shows what the women would earn per hour at the offer.Leader
  3. Fair work planTasks go to members by skill and free time, each with a reason. A declined task moves to someone else.Leader and members
  4. Production and qualityThe work is split into stages. The leader records which pieces passed and which need rework.Leader
  5. Show-your-work pay slipEach member sees pieces × rate in plain words, in Hindi or English, and can ask “Maine kitna kamaya, aur kyun?”Member
  6. Group approves payPay is settled only when the leader and enough members approve, and no dispute is open.Whole group

Four rules we build by

People decideThe AI suggests. The group approves the price, the plan and the pay.
Money maths is exactPrice and pay come from fixed, tested Decimal code, never from the AI.
Honest recordsEvery action goes into a hash-chained ledger, so edits to history are detected.
No false claimsWe claim no income gains we haven’t measured.
